Privacy Policy for ouisi.co

Effective November 2, 2026

This policy covers the website at ouisi.co, run by OuiSi Inc., 40 Buck Road, Stone Ridge, New York 12484. The short version: this website collects nothing about you. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, shows no ads and uses no tracking of any kind.

What this website collects

Nothing. There are no accounts to create, no forms that store what you type, and no newsletter sign-up on ouisi.co. The pages, pictures, fonts and the small script that runs the menu and the card lookup all come from ouisi.co itself, not from other companies, so visiting ouisi.co does not tell any advertising, analytics or social network that you were here.

What our web host records

ouisi.co is hosted by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like every web host, GitHub sees your IP address when your browser asks for a page; GitHub says it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Those records are GitHub's, kept under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. OuiSi does not receive them and cannot see them.

Cookies and tracking

ouisi.co sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Because nothing tracks you here, there is nothing to switch off: a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal from your browser is respected simply because there is nothing for it to stop.

If you write to us

If you email info@ouisi.co, we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you. Our email runs on Google Workspace. Ask us and we'll delete your messages.

Where our links go

Some links on ouisi.co take you to other places, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Our shop at shop.ouisi.co, where the Photo Cards are sold, runs on Shopify. See the shop's privacy policy.
  • The OuiSi Nature portal at go.ouisi.co, where looking up a card number takes you, is a separate OuiSi site that uses Google Analytics. Its privacy policy is the shop's, linked above.
  • Our apps, OuiSi Daily and OuiSi Photologue, have their own policies: OuiSi Daily and OuiSi Photologue. Their beta versions install through Apple's TestFlight.
  • Social networks: Facebook, X, Instagram, Pinterest and LinkedIn, under their own policies.

Children

OuiSi is played at every age. This website collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Selling or sharing

We don't sell or share personal information from this website. We have none to sell or share.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Questions: write to info@ouisi.co.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
