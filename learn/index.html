OuiSi QR Codes

Using your phone or tablet, open the camera and scan the QR code on the back of any photo card. Your phone will then prompt you to open a OuiSi link.

You’ll arrive at a page dedicated to the photo card, the full-blown original photo, and to the photographer who took the picture.

Or, you can enter the number indicated on the back of your photo card.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
