Play with curiosity

Explore with OuiSi Nature

Creatures, critters, and natural wonders

Each Photo Card in OuiSi Nature is a snippet of a larger one that highlights a particular pattern, shape or color. That invites curiosity and the question "what am I looking at?" The answer is found in the OuiSi Nature portal, where players can learn about the nature depicted and about the conservationist who took the photo.

OuiSi QR Codes

Access the OuiSi Nature Learning Portal

With your phone, scan the QR code on the back of any photo card, and open the OuiSi link that appears. Or, you can enter the number indicated on the back of your photo card in the box below.

Original or Nature?

OuiSi Nature and OuiSi Original are playable standalone, but they ALSO work beautifully together. Patterns, shapes and colors repeat throughout nature and the everyday world. Playing these two sets together reminds us that we're always in nature in some way or another.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
