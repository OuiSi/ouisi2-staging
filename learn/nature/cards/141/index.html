Iceberg Reflected

Location
Neko Harbour, Antarctica
Date
February 21, 2017
Photograph byCamille Seaman

Description

Icebergs have amazing lives. This one is close to the end, it's been whittled away by sun, waves and wind. On a calm day like this the reflections emphasize the beauty.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
