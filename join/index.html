Open OuiSi Photologue

Don't have the app yet?

  1. Install OuiSi Photologue from TestFlight. It's a free public beta.
  2. Open it once and sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Come back and tap your invite link again. You'll land in the Photologue.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
