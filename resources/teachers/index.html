Teaching guides and plans

Beta These guides are new. Try one and tell us how it went.

Each guide is a single page. It tells you what to say, what to watch for, and how to adjust for the children in front of you, with a grade ladder at the top so you can see at a glance what your class would actually do. Print it, or keep it open on a phone. You need one OuiSi Photo-Card set and nothing else.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
