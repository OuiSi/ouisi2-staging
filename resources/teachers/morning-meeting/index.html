OuiSi · Classroom ritual

Morning meeting

Beta
Grades K–5 Teaching 10 min Plus 2 to set out — clearing is step 3 Class any size Set any Use daily, all year
set out 2′   Everyone takes one 2′ Round the circle 6′ Make one link 2′
Goal
A repeatable opening that gets every child to speak once, early, without anyone having to think of something to say.
Materials
One OuiSi Photo-Card set. About thirty cards, spread in the middle of the circle.
K–1
Two steps, six minutes. Drop the linking — about 8 min
Grades 2–5
All three steps, ten minutes — the full 10 min

1 · Everyone takes one

2 min

Children sit in a circle with the cards spread face up in the middle. Each child takes one.

Take the card you want. Do not explain it.

No criteria. On a Monday it might be how the weekend went, on a Thursday it might be the color. Both are fine, and not specifying is what keeps this fresh for 180 mornings.

2 · Round the circle

6 min

Go around once. Each child says one sentence and passes on. You go first, every time.

My card is ______ and I picked it because ______.

The only rule to hold.

One sentence. If you want to say more, find me at break.

The sentence cap is what makes this fit in ten minutes with 25 children. Hold it kindly and absolutely, and children stop testing it within a week.

Any child may pass. A pass gets the same nod as an answer.

3 · Make one link

2 min

Who is holding a card that connects to the one next to them? Put them together in the middle.

Two or three pairs, no more. Then collect the cards and start the day. Stopping while they still want to go on is the point.

Watch for

  • It growing. This works because it is short. The first time it runs twenty minutes it stops being a ritual and becomes a lesson you will start skipping.
  • The same children passing every day. Fine for a week or two. After that, catch them privately and ask which card they would have taken.
  • Never require disclosure. I just liked it is a complete sentence and a complete answer.
  • Card hoarding. Children find favorites. Let them, and reshuffle the thirty you put out every Monday.

Adapt

  • Closing circle — same shape, one change: take the card that matches how today went.
  • Multilingual learners — the sentence frame carries them. Any language, or point and say one word.
  • Speech and language — holding the card up is a turn. No words needed.
  • Anxiety — tell them privately they can pass all week with no questions asked. Most start speaking by Thursday.
  • Field trips and nature walks — take ten cards along. Same ritual, outside, at the halfway point.
Curriculum links. Speaking once in a group, every day · Giving a reason for a choice · Listening for a connection to a neighbor's contribution · A predictable routine every child can enter.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
