Passionflower Vine

Passiflora vitifolia

Location
Barro Colorado Island, Panama
Date
November 14, 2017
Photograph byChristian Ziegler

Description

These gorgeous vigorous growing passionflower vines are native to Central America. The flowers only stay open for a day so it is likely to only get a handful of blooms at a time. The species ranges from Nicaragua to Venezuela and Peru. Its red flowers are pollinated by hummingbirds, and its fruits are greenish-yellow berries 5 to 8 cm long, marked with white lines.
