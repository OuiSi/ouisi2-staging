Camouflaged Caterpillar

Location
Manu National Park, Peru
Photograph byChristian Ziegler

Description

These creatures can camouflage themselves to avoid being detected by other animals. Manu National Park, created in 1973 and a World Heritage Site since 1987, spans the whole basin of the Manu River, from cold grasslands above 4,000 meters down to the Amazon lowlands. More than 1,300 species of butterflies have been recorded there.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
