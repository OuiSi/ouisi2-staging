Mouth of White-⁠spotted Rose Anemone

Urticina lofotensis

Location
Channel Islands, CA, USA
Date
April 29, 2016
Photograph byKate Vylet

Description

This white-spotted rose anemone unwittingly caught a ball of sand debris in its mouth while being blown about in the surge. Its white spots are smooth bumps set in rows up its red column, and they usually hold no sand or shell. It grows to about 10 cm across and lives mostly below the tides, on exposed rocky coast from southeast Alaska to San Diego.
