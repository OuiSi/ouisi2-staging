Bio

Lenz Lim is a wildlife and macro photographer based in Singapore with years of photography experience. Watching endless nature documentaries fuelled his interest in wildlife photography and what drives his passion for exploring and sharing any discoveries with others. Many go by their lives not noticing the beauty of nature around them and the little critters that share this Earth with us.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
