Kate Vylet

Underwater photographer/videographer and scientific diver

Bio

Kate Vylet is an underwater photographer, marine scientist, and divemaster anchored in Monterey Bay, California. Her photography and videography explore California's underwater coast capturing marine life hidden below the surface. With a background in ecology and specialising in California's iconic kelp forests, she focuses her work through a lens of science to bring light to the ocean's breathtaking beauty and incredible intricacy. By sharing stories of our underwater worlds, Kate hopes to inspire awareness and action for the vibrant life found on this blue planet.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
