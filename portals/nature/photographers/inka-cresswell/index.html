Inka Cresswell

Wildlife Filmmaker, B.Sc. Marine Biologist, Underwater Photographer / Researcher, Rebreather/Tech Diver, HSE Media Diver

Bio

Inka Cresswell is a Bristol-based wildlife filmmaker and underwater camera operator. She holds a B.Sc. in Marine Biology and M.A. in Wildlife Filmmaking from the University of the West of England. Inka’s goal is to be able to use her background in marine science to create inspiring ocean conservation films that inform and educate the next generation, driving real conservation impact. Inka also works as a social influencer in the diving industry and gives public talks on ocean conservation, conservation filmmaking and marine topics to inspire more girls to become involved in the wildlife filmmaking and STEM industries. With over 10 years of SCUBA diving experience, it's fair to say Inka is obsessed with our oceans. Inka is a PADI Open Water Scuba Diver Instructor, TDI technical diver and has experience in underwater field research including AAUS NAUI scientific diver training.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
