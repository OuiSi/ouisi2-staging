"What's a OuiSi?"

OuiSi's mission is to get you seeing with fresh eyes. In our games, each Photo Card is a snippet of a larger photo or artwork, framed in such a way that it gets players asking "what is that?" In our apps, the gameplay asks you to notice the often-overlooked patterns, shapes and colors of your daily life.

The physical games

This looks like that.

OuiSi makes award-winning sets of Visually Connecting Photo Cards that "match" based on similar colors, shapes and patterns. Each set comes with a Guidebook full of collaborative games and creative activities that ignite fun, creativity and conversation, regardless of age.

The Photo Cards

Original or Nature?

OuiSi Original Photo Cards capture small marvels from the everyday, while OuiSi Nature celebrates nature's incredible shapes, patterns and colors. And, every Nature card has a QR code on the back that opens OuiSi Nature's learning portal.

While the two sets are different, they can be used together to connect between nature and everyday life.

Learning Portals

Play with curiosity

OuiSi Nature and our museum partner sets like OuiSi Getty have QR codes on the back of each Photo Card and Art Card. These codes link to learning portals, where you can learn more (and see more!) about what you're seeing on each card.

Why we made OuiSi

We believe that everything is connected. That life is a richer experience when we are curious about each other.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
