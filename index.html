How to play Learn

This looks like that.

OuiSi gets you seeing, playing and thinking differently. We've spent years making award-winning sets of Visually Connecting Photo Cards – "this looks like that" – with Guidebooks full of games that ignite fun, creativity and conversation, regardless of age.

Now, we are thoughtfully introducing OuiSi to your phones and desktop, with creative ways of using tech to magnify the in-real-life experience. Meet OuiSi Daily and OuiSi Photologue.

The Photo Cards

Original or Nature?

OuiSi Original Photo Cards capture small marvels from the everyday, while OuiSi Nature celebrates nature's incredible shapes, patterns and colors. And, every Nature card has a QR code on the back that opens OuiSi Nature's learning portal.

Each set comes with 210 Photo Cards and a Guidebook full of games, in a sturdy, beautiful box. While the two sets are different, they can be used together to connect between nature and everyday life. In fact, you can bundle them and save $10.

What's OuiSi?

Pronounced "We-See," OuiSi translates to "Yes Yes" from other languages. It's a nod to our belief that pictures are a language nearly all of us speak. OuiSi is based in the NY Hudson Valley.
